# Changelog

## 0.1.7 — 2026-10-14

First public, Windows x64 release.

- Hold-to-talk and hands-free dictation with automatic paste.
- Topmost recording bar with four positions and audio animation.
- Durable pending audio, background retries and locally saved transcripts.
- Delete audio after successful speech recognition.
- Optional smart writing and vocabulary correction through your own Groq key.
- Encrypted local credential storage and quiet Windows sign-in startup.
- Updated Electron runtime, audited dependencies and publication checks.

macOS, Linux and iOS are not supported yet. Contributions are welcome.
